Vredenburg's Trusted Name in Eye Care

For more than half a century, Weck & Danner has been the name Vredenburg families turn to for their eyes. What started as a small local practice has grown alongside the town itself, but the approach has never changed, every patient gets a proper, unhurried consultation, not a rushed once-over.

We've since brought in modern diagnostic equipment and an expanded range of lenses and frames, but the reason people keep coming back — and keep sending their children and grandchildren to us — is simple. We take the time to actually understand what your eyes need, and we stand behind the advice we give.

It's why generations of local families still call Weck & Danner their optometrist.

What We Do

Complete Eye Care Under One Roof

From routine eye exams to licence screenings, every service starts with the same thorough, unhurried approach.

Comprehensive Eye Examinations

Regular eye tests do more than update your prescription. They can catch serious health problems early. Your exam can include retinal photography (a photo of the back of your eye) and tonometry, which detects abnormal eye pressure. Every exam is tailored to your needs, so you leave seeing clearly and comfortably.

Glaucoma Testing

Protect your vision from glaucoma damage with regular complete eye tests. Our comprehensive glaucoma exam checks five factors before any diagnosis is made.

Contact Lens Consultation

It starts with an eye health assessment to confirm you can wear contact lenses. Once you're cleared, we fit you and talk through the lens options that suit your lifestyle.

Paediatric Services

Eye tests for young children, matched to their age: image perception, colour vision, vision-related learning difficulties, coordination, eye muscle control and focusing.

Drivers Licence Screening

Save time at the Traffic Department and let us do your screening. Bring a valid ID. The screening follows National Department of Transport requirements, so it's accepted.

Skippers Licence Screening

Coastal and Offshore skipper candidates must pass the prescribed SAMSA tests at a SAMSA office. We can issue the eye test certificate you'll need, as a registered health professional.

Comfort for Dry Eyes and Contact Lens Wearers

Long days on screens, wind, dust and air conditioning can leave eyes feeling dry and tired. Contact lens wearers feel it even more.

We stock lubricating eye drops and contact lens solutions from trusted brands, so you can pick up what you need while you're here. Not sure what suits you? Ask us at your next visit and we'll point you to the right one.
